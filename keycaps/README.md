# KLP Lamé keycaps for TOTEM

[Download the 40-key STEP](klp-lame-sprued-40.step) for Kailh Choc v1.
The set has 10 Saddle Tilted, 8 Saddle, and 1 Saddle Homing per side,
plus 2 spare Saddle caps. Both sides use the same upstream tilted model.

The caps form one solid measuring 93.50 × 142.49 × 9.64 mm. Neighboring
skirts are joined by Ø1.5 × 2.5 mm sprues on a 19 × 18 mm pitch. Snip the
sprues after printing. The STEP was checked as one valid solid but has not
been test-printed or approved by JLC3DP.

Adapted from [KLP Lamé by braindefender](https://github.com/braindefender/KLP-Lame-Keycaps/tree/8e4b89b484cafe3bcaced6b459a4e29660991a40)
by arranging the stock keycaps and fusing sprues between them. The STEP is
distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
