# KLP Lamé keycaps for TOTEM

Upload each STEP separately for Kailh Choc v1. The
[JLC3DP connected-parts guide](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/213-Connected-Parts-Printing-Guide)
limits each file to 10 small parts joined as one shell. Each file below
contains 9 caps in a 3 × 3 grid and was checked as one valid solid.

| STEP | Saddle Tilted | Saddle | Saddle Homing | Size (X × Y × Z) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| [Set 1](klp-lame-set1.step) | 9 | 0 | 0 | 55.50 × 52.50 × 9.64 mm |
| [Set 2](klp-lame-set2.step) | 9 | 0 | 0 | 55.50 × 52.50 × 9.64 mm |
| [Set 3](klp-lame-set3.step) | 4 | 5 | 0 | 55.50 × 52.50 × 9.64 mm |
| [Set 4](klp-lame-set4.step) | 0 | 9 | 0 | 55.49 × 52.49 × 6.08 mm |
| [Set 5](klp-lame-set5.step) | 0 | 7 | 2 | 55.49 × 52.49 × 6.08 mm |
| **Total** | **22** | **21** | **2** | **45 caps** |

Sets 1 and 2 are identical; order two copies of that plate if the uploader
combines identical files. Neighboring skirts are joined by Ø1.5 × 2.5 mm
sprues on a 19 × 18 mm pitch. The original thin sprues are retained so
they can be snipped off after printing. JLC3DP also lists a connection
thickness requirement greater than 1.5 mm; acceptance of these thin
sprues has not been confirmed. The sets have not been test-printed.

Adapted from [KLP Lamé by braindefender](https://github.com/braindefender/KLP-Lame-Keycaps/tree/8e4b89b484cafe3bcaced6b459a4e29660991a40)
by arranging the stock keycaps and fusing sprues between them. The STEP is
